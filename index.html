01 — ABOUT

Fundamental chemistry.
Functional outcomes.

I am a researcher in organic and physical organic chemistry, with a focus on how light can be used to control molecular transformations and material properties. My research experience spans photoremovable protecting groups, wavelength-selective photochemistry, fluorescence-based sensing, and dynamic covalent systems.

I completed my PhD at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, working on wavelength-tunable photoremovable protecting groups for biomedical and materials applications. I am currently a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Chemistry at the University of Warwick.

This website is a research overview. Please see the publications and professional links below for further details.
